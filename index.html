Welcome to Da Nang

Da Nang is one of the most beautiful coastal cities in Vietnam. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, delicious food and amazing landscapes.

Top 3 Places to Visit

  • My Khe Beach - A beautiful beach with white sand.
  • Golden Bridge - A famous bridge held by giant hands.
  • Marble Mountains - A group of limestone and marble hills.
